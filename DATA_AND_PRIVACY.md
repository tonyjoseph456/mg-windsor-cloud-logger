# Data & Privacy Guide

This document explains in plain English **what data this app saves**, **where it lives** (on your phone vs. in the Cloudflare database), and **how each user's car data is kept separate and private** when sharing the app with friends.

---

## Quick Summary

- **Your MG iSMART password is NEVER saved in our database.**
- **Your 4-digit MG Security PIN is NEVER saved in our database.**
- **Private Shortcut & CarPlay links contain NO phone number, NO password, and NO VIN.** Instead, each car gets a random private key (`wk_...`).
- **Strict separation between users:** Every trip, charging session, notification, and push alert is tagged with only your car's VIN and protected by your private app token (`Authorization: Bearer ...`). Friends using the same server cannot view, edit, or trigger actions on your car.

---

## 1. What Stays ONLY on Your Device (Phone / Browser)

On iPhone PWA this is stored in Safari `localStorage` for this app; in native Expo builds it uses encrypted device Keychain / Keystore (`expo-secure-store`).

| What is stored locally | Why the app needs it | Shared with other users? |
|---|---|---|
| **MG phone number, password & 4-digit PIN** (`mg_ismart_live_session_v2`) | Kept on your phone so the app can open your dashboard and automatically renew an expired MG token without asking you to type everything again. | **No** (never stored in D1) |
| **MG temporary login tokens (`uid` & `token`) & VIN** | Used to fetch live battery, location, and car status for your car. | **No** |
| **Invite code & private app keys** (`mg_pwa_saved_access_code_v1`, `mg_pwa_license_token_v1`, `mg_pwa_api_token_v1`, `mg_pwa_webhook_key_v1`) | Proves to the Cloudflare backend that your phone owns this car and lets your Shortcut/CarPlay links authenticate without exposing phone/VIN/password. | **No** |
| **Cached car snapshot, active/recent trips & charge logs** | Lets the app open quickly offline and preserves active trip/charge tracking between screen refreshes. | **No** |
| **App settings** (car colour, text size scale, Google Maps API key, optional Google Sheets config, Live Activity toggle, admin flag) | Remembers your visual preferences and optional integrations on your device. | **No** |

## 2. What Is Saved in the Cloudflare Database (D1 & Worker KV)

To sync history across your devices, support CarPlay webhooks, run smart charging notifications, and isolate users cleanly, the Cloudflare Worker stores only the data below in **Cloudflare D1** (and optional Worker KV mirror).

### A. Account & Access Control

| Table | What it stores | Why it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| **`authorized_users`** | Phone number, car VIN, car display name, status (`active` or `blocked`), browser/device user-agent string, invite code used, and last-active timestamp. | Controls who can use this private instance and lets an admin revoke access at any time. |
| **`access_codes`** | One-time invite codes generated by the admin, optional label/name, and whether/when a code was claimed. | Lets you invite friends without making the app public to everyone. |
| **`access_logs`** | Timestamps and outcomes of sign-in and authorization checks (granted / denied / blocked). | Basic access audit trail for the admin dashboard. |

### B. Private Session & Push Keys (`kv_store`)

| Entry | What it stores | What is **NOT** here |
|---|---|---|
| **`mg_session:<VIN>`** | Current MG temporary session (`uid`, `token`), VIN, and phone number. | **No MG password. No 4-digit Control PIN.** |
| **`api_token:<token>`** | Random app API token mapped strictly to your VIN. | Not shared across VINs. |
| **`webhook_key:<key>`** (and `vin_webhook_key:<VIN>`) | Random secret key (`wk_...`) used in your Siri, Lock Screen widget, and CarPlay links, mapped strictly to your VIN. | Rotated cleanly if you tap **Reset private links**. |
| **`push_subs:<VIN>`** | Apple/Web Push notification endpoint + encryption keys (`p256dh`, `auth`) for sending alerts to your Lock Screen. | Only used for your own car's VIN. |

### C. Your Car History (Strictly Isolated by VIN)

Every row in these D1 tables belongs to one `vin`, and every API request verifies that your bearer token matches that exact `vin`:

| Table | What it stores |
|---|---|
| **`trips`** | Trip start/end time, distance (km), duration, battery start/end %, energy used (kWh), efficiency (km/kWh), odometer, GPS coordinates, and street/city names. |
| **`charging_sessions` & `charges`** | Plug-in / unplug timestamps, start/end SOC %, energy added (kWh), estimated charging power (kW), duration, and charger summary details. |
| **`notifications`** | In-app history of sent alerts (trip started/completed, charging started/paused/resumed/completed). |

## 3. How Privacy-Safe Siri, Widget & CarPlay Links Work (Option A)

Under **Option A** (zero credentials and zero PIN stored on the server):

1. **Car Command links** (Unlock, Lock, Climate On, Climate Off) look like:
   ```text
   https://<worker-host>/api/command/unlock?key=<your-private-key>&pin=<your-4-digit-pin>
   ```
   - There is **no phone number**, **no MG password**, and **no VIN** in the URL.
   - Your **4-digit MG Security PIN** stays only inside your iPhone Shortcut link. When you run it, the Worker forwards the command to MG and never saves the PIN in D1 or KV.

2. **CarPlay Trip links** (Car connects / Car disconnects) look like:
   ```text
   https://<worker-host>/api/trip/start?key=<your-private-key>&format=text
   https://<worker-host>/api/trip/stop?key=<your-private-key>&format=text
   ```
   - No phone number, password, or VIN appears in the URL — only your random `key`.

3. **Resetting compromised links:**
   - If you ever accidentally share a Shortcut screenshot or URL, open **Profile → Siri, widgets & CarPlay** and tap **Reset private links**.
   - This immediately replaces your old `key` with a new random key (`wk_...`) so old links stop working right away.

4. **When MG expires your temporary session token:**
   - Because our database does not store your MG password, a Shortcut link cannot re-log into MG by itself if MG expires your session token after extended inactivity (`401 MG_SESSION_EXPIRED`).
   - Opening the app on your phone refreshes your session automatically using the credentials stored on your device.

## 4. How Users Are Kept Strictly Isolated

- Every authenticated app request (`/api/trips`, `/api/charges`, `/api/charging-history`, `/api/notifications`, `/api/push-*`, `/api/webhook-key*`, `/api/deregister-car`) requires `Authorization: Bearer <your-token>`, which resolves server-side to **your VIN** (cannot be overridden with another VIN in query params or body — mismatches are rejected with HTTP `403`).
- Every webhook (`/api/command/*`, `/api/trip/start`, `/api/trip/stop`) resolves only from your private `key` (`wk_...`) to your single VIN.
- Background cron charging & trip checks iterate per VIN independently, check `authorized_users` status, and deliver Web Push alerts only to `push_subs:<that VIN>`.
- Revoking a user in the Admin Console (blocking them) invalidates their active app tokens immediately.

---

## 5. Logging Out vs. Wiping Your Cloud Data

- **Log Out:** Removes your local login session, cached dashboard snapshot, API token, and private link key from your phone/browser so nobody else picking up that device stays signed in.
- **Wipe Everything & Log Out:** Tells the server (`/api/deregister-car` with `clearHistory: true`) to delete your cloud MG session, push notification subscriptions, and stored trips, charges, charging history, and notifications for your VIN, then clears all app keys from your device.
